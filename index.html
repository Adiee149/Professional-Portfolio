The Engineering Perspective

I started my journey using data to answer critical business questions. Over the past 3+ years as a Power BI Senior Developer, I've designed enterprise-scale solutions for over 100 users and complex dimensional models.

But building dashboards taught me a fundamental truth: analytics is only as good as the pipeline feeding it.

My focus has naturally shifted upstream. I am increasingly interested in the systems behind the data—optimizing SQL queries for 50% performance gains, orchestrating pipelines with Power Automate to eliminate manual work, and managing data quality. I am deliberately expanding my skillset in Python and cloud platforms to build robust data engineering architectures at scale.
